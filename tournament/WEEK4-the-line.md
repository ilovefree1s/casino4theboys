# Week 4 idea: The Line (knockout week)

Decided with Justin on 2026-11-21. Not built yet.

- One wallet for all games again (like weeks 1-2): fresh $5,000 every day.
- Every day has a line: the profit you need by midnight. It rises through the week.
- Everyone has ONE lifeline. Finishing a day under the line, or missing a day entirely, uses it.
- Miss again with no lifeline left: knocked out.
- No hot games.
- Markers stay: up to 3, same rules as the money weeks.

Still open:
- What a knocked-out player can do (nothing / play for fun / walk-ups at the survivors).
- The line numbers per day (set from real week 1-3 results).
- Winner if several survive Saturday (proposal: most money on Saturday), or if everyone left falls the same night (proposal: closest to the line).
