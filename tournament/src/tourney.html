THE TOURNAMENT
SUNDAY 12:00 AM – SATURDAY 11:59 PM · EASTERN · FRESH $5,000 EVERY DAY
  1. A fresh $5,000 every day. At midnight your money resets and you start over in The Basement. Same tables and limits as Campaign. Rooms open with your bankroll: Main Floor at $10,000, High Limit at $50,000, The Salon at $250,000, Whale Room at $1,000,000.
  2. Your best 3 days win the week. Each day's score is what you made: your money at midnight, minus what you owe on markers, minus the $5,000 you started with. At the end of the week your 3 best days are added up, and the biggest total is the champion. If you play fewer than 3 days, the days you played are what count.
  3. 🔥 Hot game of the day. Every day one game is the hot game, and whatever you win there pays double. The extra lands in your bankroll when you leave the table, and it's counted toward your day the whole time you're playing.
  4. Markers: the house gives you $5,000, and you owe $7,500 back. You can take one once you're down to $1,000 or when you go broke, and hold up to 3 unpaid at a time. Whatever you still owe at midnight comes off that day's score, then it's wiped. Go broke holding 3 unpaid and you're out for the day; you're back with $5,000 at midnight.
  5. The board updates once a day, at midnight. THE WEEK shows everyone's best-3 total as of last midnight, and YESTERDAY shows what each player made that day, with a daily winner. During the day, only you can see your own money.
  6. One phone. Your name is locked to this phone, and your PIN gets you back in if it ever forgets you.
