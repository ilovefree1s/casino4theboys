PAYS & RULES

25 lines, paid left to right from the first reel. Amounts are for your current total bet.

WILD

The ACE medallion lands on reels 2, 3 and 4 and stands in for every symbol except the basket.

HOLD & SPIN

Every basket carries a prize: 1× to 15× your bet, or the Par, Birdie or Eagle jackpot. Land 6 or more baskets anywhere and they lock in place. You get 3 spins where only the empty spots spin. Every new basket locks too and sets you back to 3 spins. When the spins run out you collect every basket on the screen.

Fill all 15 spots and you hit the ACE jackpot on top.

Play testing only: free-play chips, never the campaign or the tournament.
