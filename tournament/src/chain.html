PAYS & RULES

25 lines, paid left to right from the first reel. Amounts are for your current total bet.

WILD

The ACE medallion lands on reels 2, 3 and 4 and stands in for every symbol except the basket.

CHAIN RATTLE BONUS

3, 4 or 5 baskets anywhere pay 5×, 15× or 50× your bet up front. Then you putt: stop the needle in the green. Make the 10 footer for Par, the 20 footer for Birdie and the 30 footer for the Ace. The green shrinks and the needle speeds up each time. A miss ends the bonus, and you keep everything you made.

Play testing only: free-play chips, never the campaign or the tournament.
