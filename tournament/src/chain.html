PAYS & RULES

25 lines, paid left to right from the first reel. Your bet covers all 25 lines, so each line plays 1/25 of it. Amounts below are for your current bet.

ACE is wild on reels 2, 3 and 4. It stands in for everything except the basket.

Chain Rattle bonus. 3, 4 or 5 baskets anywhere pay 5×, 15× or 50× your bet up front. Then you putt: stop the needle in the green. Make the 10 footer for Par, then the 20 footer for Birdie, then the 30 footer for the Ace. The green gets smaller and the needle gets faster each time. Miss and the bonus ends, but you keep everything you made.

Play testing only. It plays with free-play chips and never touches the campaign or the tournament.
