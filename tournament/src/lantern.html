PAYS & RULES

25 lines, paid left to right from the first reel. Amounts are for your current total bet.

WILD

The chili lands on reels 2, 3 and 4 and stands in for every symbol except the lantern.

HOLD & SPIN

Every lantern carries a prize: 1× to 15× your bet, or the Mini, Minor or Major jackpot. Land 6 or more lanterns anywhere and they lock in place. You get 3 spins where only the empty spots spin. Every new lantern locks too and sets you back to 3 spins. When the spins run out you collect every lantern on the screen.

Fill all 15 spots and you win the GRAND on top.
