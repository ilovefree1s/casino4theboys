# Ultimate Texas Hold'em settlement cases, shared by both builds.
#
# The Compose engine (UltimateHoldemRules.settle) and the web engine
# (window.UTH.settle) are two hand-written copies of the same maths. This file
# is the referee: HoldemParityTest.kt runs it against Kotlin, web/parity.html
# runs it against the JavaScript, and both must agree with every line here.
#
# Cards are rank + suit: 2-9, T, J, Q, K, A and s h d c.
#
# player | dealer | board | ante blind play trips folded | outcome qualified anteReturn blindReturn playReturn tripsReturn | player hand | dealer hand
#
# Returns are gross — stake plus winnings — so a push returns exactly the stake.
# "q"/"nq" is whether the dealer opened; "fold" in the stakes column is a fold.

As Ad | Kh Kc | Ah 9d 2c 4s 7h | 10 10 40 0 -    | win  q  20 10 80 0    | Three of a Kind | Pair

2s 5s | Kd Qc | 8s Js Ks 3h 4d | 10 10 20 0 -    | win  q  20 40 40 0    | Flush           | Pair
